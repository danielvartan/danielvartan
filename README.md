### Olá, I'm Daniel! 👋

I'm a Media Manager at [Talent Marcel](https://talent.com.br/) ([Publicis Groupe](https://www.publicisgroupe.com)) and an [R](https://www.r-project.org/)(esearch) Collaborator at the University of São Paulo ([USP](https://www.usp.br/)). My work focuses on spatial analysis, [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system) modeling, and computational approaches to marketing, social, and public health challenges.

My surname is [KACHvartanian](https://www.armenianinstitute.org.uk/new-page-3) (go ahead, try saying it), but I go by Vartanian to spare everyone the linguistic workout. I grew up between the São Paulo coast and the city, in an Armenian diaspora household. I love building open-source tools, being part of active communities, and working with the [R](https://www.r-project.org/), [Python](https://www.python.org/), and [NetLogo](https://www.netlogo.org/) programming languages. Outside of work, I'm likely watching a good movie, seeing friends, wandering through new places, or tinkering with some obscure new thing that may or may not go anywhere.
